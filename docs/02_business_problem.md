# Business Problem

NYC receives large volumes of 311 service requests covering different
agencies, service categories, locations and operational processes.

Raw request volume alone does not tell city operations where intervention
is most valuable.

Operational managers need to understand:

- where demand is concentrated
- where unresolved requests are accumulating
- which service categories experience persistent delays
- which geographic areas repeatedly generate service demand
- whether delay patterns are concentrated within particular agency × service combinations
- whether recurring temporal or geographic patterns exist
- where further operational investigation should begin

The analytical objective is therefore to transform individual service requests
into defensible measures of demand, backlog, resolution reliability,
service burden and operational attention.

Agency comparisons must account for differences in the services handled.
A long resolution time cannot automatically be interpreted as poor agency
performance without understanding the underlying complaint category and
request lifecycle.
